DialUP is a bridge between UPnP/DLNA media renderers and casting. It finds the renderers on your Wi-Fi network and shows each one as a cast target in the real YouTube and YouTube Music apps. It then looks up the audio with yt-dlp, prepares it with FFmpeg and serves it to the speaker.

This version casts audio only.

<b>Features</b>
• Native casting control from YouTube and YouTube Music: seeking, volume, next and previous, autoplay, shuffle, repeat and track details
• Three playback modes: Streaming (starts quickly), Direct (least work for the phone) and Cached (smoothest seeking on the speaker)
• Gapless track changes on renderers that support them
• Hide renderers you don't want in the cast menu, such as TVs, and add a renderer by its address
• Recovers from short Wi-Fi drops without ending the cast
• Updates yt-dlp from within the app, with a choice of stable or nightly builds

<b>Privacy</b>
No account, no server of its own and no analytics. Everything runs locally. DialUP never signs in to Google; yt-dlp looks up the audio anonymously, and the speaker only receives a link to your phone on your own network.

<b>Requirements</b>
Android 8.0 or newer, a UPnP/DLNA renderer on the same Wi-Fi network, and the YouTube or YouTube Music app.
